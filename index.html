About

What is The Corpus?

The Corpus is a curated body of writing: original poems, borrowed literary echoes, and research notes arranged by mood, collection, and recurring obsessions — body, memory, myth, violence, tenderness, faith, absence, and thought.

Written by Hand

Original works by Dhruva Burande — private mythologies, visceral fragments, elegies, indictments, and devotional ruptures.

Atlas

World-literature and former Borrowed Ledger poems organized by country and poet.

Eras

The same wider reading field arranged chronologically, so readers can enter by period instead of place.

Open Scholarship

Research in Literature and Philosophy

A lightweight global index of influential open-access scholarship in literature and philosophy. The Corpus stores metadata and direct PDF links only—not the papers themselves.
